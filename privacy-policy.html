Cystera

PCOS, PCOD & PMOS record · Privacy Policy

Summary

Cystera is an offline health record. It does not have an account, does not show ads, and the release build does not request any network permission. Everything the app writes stays on your phone, encrypted.

Data we collect

None. Cystera does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no account. The data you enter — cycles, symptoms, medications, measurements, lab values, reminders and notes — is stored locally on your device only.

Where your data is stored

Your record is encrypted with SQLCipher under a random 32-byte key. That key is wrapped by a key derived from your PIN together with a 32-byte device secret that never leaves the phone's secure hardware (the keystore / TEE). A backup file you export is sealed with a passphrase you choose, at a deliberately higher derivation cost.

The language preference is stored separately so the lock screen can display words before the encrypted record is unlocked.

Permissions

The release build requests only the permissions needed to function: storage (for the encrypted database and exported files), biometric / device credential (for the lock gate), and notifications (for the reminders you arm). It does not request INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. A build-time guard fails the build if anything would let a release artifact reach a network.

Backups

A backup is a file you choose to export and share. It is encrypted end-to-end and can only be restored by this app on a device that can unlock it. The doctor report (PDF / CSV) is the one part of Cystera built to leave the phone and is therefore not encrypted, because a clinician must be able to open it. The share sheet states this when you export it.

Third-party code

Cystera uses the Flutter framework, the Android platform, SQLCipher via sqflite_sqlcipher, and the Android keystore. No third party receives any data from the app, because the app sends none.
